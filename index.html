Etch-a-Sketch

A small canvas. Make something with it.

16 × 16

Draw by moving

Move your pointer across the squares to draw. On a touch screen, drag a finger.

Darken mode adds 10% black with each visit. Ten visits make a square fully black.

Keyboard: focus the canvas, move with arrow keys and paint with Space.
